# *Product Management in Practice* Book Notes

These notes cover material from [Product Management in Practice](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/product-management-in/9781098119720/), 2nd Edition
by Matt LeMay. The book is a practical guide to navigating the messy realities of product management. Drawing on real-world experience, LeMay shows product managers how to work effectively with teams, stakeholders, and uncertainty, while focusing less on rigid frameworks and more on communication, collaboration, and sound decision-making.
