# *Business Analysis* Book Notes

These notes cover material from [Business Analysis](https://shop.bcs.org/page/detail/business-analysis/?SF1=work_exact&ST1=BUSINESSANALYSIS4), 4th edition, by Debra Paul, James Cadle, Malcolm Eva, Craig Rollason, and Jonathan Hunsley. The book is a practical guide to the tools, techniques, and principles of effective business analysis. It covers stakeholder engagement, requirements, process improvement, and business change, making it a valuable resource for analysts and professionals involved in organisational improvement.
