# Notes and Projects for the IBM Product Manager Professional Certificate

Course notes and projects for the [IBM Product Manager Professional Certificate](https://www.ibm.com/training/badge/ibm-product-manager-professional-certificate).

This course is a 3-month, self-paced programme aimed at reinforcing foundational skills for product management and product ownership. Combining the IBM Product Manager Professional Certificate with three selected modules from the IBM Product Owner Professional Certificate, this custom programme integrates product strategy, market research and Agile practices with innovation tools, generative AI applications, and communication and stakeholder management. Learners develop practical skills to shape product direction, collaborate across teams and support products from concept to launch.

## Notes and Projects

- Product Management: An Introduction
- Product Management: Foundations & Stakeholder Collaboration
- Product Owner: Communications & Stakeholder Management
- Product Management: Initial Product Strategy and Plan
- Product Owner: Essential Skills and Tools for Innovation
- Product Management: Developing and Delivering a New Product
- Generative AI: Revolutionizing the Product Owner Role
- Product Management: Capstone Project
